📝 How to Apply - MPESB Group 3 Online Form 2026

  1. Registration: Visit the official MPESB/MPOnline portal and complete the online registration process.
  2. Personal Details: Enter your name, date of birth, category, address and other required personal details.
  3. Post Selection: Select the eligible post(s) and give your preferred priority where applicable.
  4. Qualification Details: Enter your educational and technical qualification details as prescribed for the selected post.
  5. Document Details: Enter the required certificate and document information wherever applicable.
  6. Photograph & Signature: Upload your recent photograph and signature in the prescribed format.
  7. Application Form: Carefully check all the information entered in the application form before final submission.
  8. Application Fee: Pay the applicable examination/application fee through the available online payment method.
  9. Final Submission: Submit the application form after verifying all details carefully.
  10. Print Application: Take a printout or save a copy of the submitted application form and payment receipt for future reference.

🙋‍♀️ MPESB Group 3 Apply Online 2026 - Faqs

❓ What are the application dates for MPESB Group 3 Recruitment 2026?

The re-open application window will be available from 13 October 2026 to 27 October 2026. Candidates can make corrections to their applications from 13 October to 28 October 2026.

❓ What is the educational qualification required for MPESB Group 3 Recruitment 2026?

The educational qualification varies according to the post and post code. Candidates must possess the relevant diploma, degree, or technical qualification prescribed for the respective post.

❓ What is the age limit for MPESB Group 3 Recruitment 2026?

The minimum age is 18 years and the maximum age is 40 years. Eligible reserved categories and other specified candidates of Madhya Pradesh may receive age relaxation up to 45 years, as per rules. The age will be calculated as of 01 January 2026.

❓ What is the total marks of the MPESB Group 3 Examination 2026?

The examination will carry a total of 200 marks, including 100 marks for general subjects and 100 marks for the relevant technical subject.

❓ What is the application fee for MPESB Group 3 Recruitment 2026?

The application fee is ₹500 per question paper for General category candidates and ₹250 per question paper for SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwD candidates belonging to Madhya Pradesh. Applicable portal charges will be additional.
