Contact Us

Contact YOJANA PORTAL for questions, suggestions, corrections, feedback and general inquiries regarding our website and information services.

Contact YOJANA PORTAL

We value your feedback, suggestions and questions. At YOJANA PORTAL, we continuously work to improve our website and provide useful information about government schemes, jobs, examinations, scholarships and educational opportunities.

If you have any questions, notice incorrect information or would like to share suggestions, please feel free to contact us through our available communication channels.

Why Contact Us?

  • General Inquiries: For questions related to our website, content and available services.
  • Information Corrections: If you find incorrect, outdated or incomplete information on any page.
  • Suggestions & Feedback: To share your ideas and recommendations for improving our website.
  • Technical Issues: To report broken links, website errors or problems accessing our content.
  • Business & Advertising Inquiries: For legitimate business proposals, advertising opportunities and collaboration requests.

Report Incorrect Information

  • We make every effort to publish accurate and useful information. However, errors or outdated details may occasionally occur.
  • If you identify any incorrect information regarding government schemes, recruitment notifications, examination dates, eligibility criteria or application procedures, please inform us.
  • To help us review your report quickly, include the page URL, the incorrect information and the correct details along with an official reference, if available.

Before Contacting Us

  • For questions regarding application status, eligibility decisions, examination results, payments or official government procedures, please contact the concerned department or authority directly.
  • YOJANA PORTAL is an independent informational website and does not process government applications, recruitment forms, examination registrations or scholarship payments.
  • Please do not share sensitive personal information, passwords, OTPs, banking details or government identification numbers through general communication channels.

Contact Information

  • For general inquiries, feedback, corrections and other communication, please use our official contact channel.
  • Email: yojanaportal.com@gmail.com
  • Website: YOJANA PORTAL
  • We aim to review genuine inquiries and respond as soon as reasonably possible. Response times may vary depending on the nature and volume of requests.

Important Notice

  • YOJANA PORTAL is not affiliated with any government department, ministry, recruitment board or official government organization.
  • Contacting us does not constitute an official application, complaint registration or request submitted to any government authority.
  • For official assistance, always refer to the relevant department’s official website and contact details.

Thank You

  • Thank you for visiting YOJANA PORTAL and being a part of our growing community.
  • Your feedback and support help us improve our platform and make useful information more accessible to everyone.
