DMCA Policy

DMCA Policy – YOJANA PORTAL

YOJANA PORTAL respects the intellectual property rights of content creators, authors, publishers and copyright owners. We are committed to reviewing legitimate copyright complaints and addressing concerns regarding unauthorized use of copyrighted material.

This policy explains how copyright owners or their authorized representatives can report allegedly infringing content published on our website and how we handle such requests.

  1. Copyright Protection

  • Original articles, written content, graphics, website design and other materials created by YOJANA PORTAL are protected by applicable intellectual property laws, unless otherwise stated.
  • Government notifications, official documents, public information and third-party materials may belong to their respective copyright owners.
  • YOJANA PORTAL does not claim ownership of third-party materials unless explicitly mentioned.
  • We encourage all visitors and content contributors to respect the intellectual property rights of others.

  1. Reporting Copyright Infringement

  • If you believe that copyrighted material owned by you has been used or published on YOJANA PORTAL without proper authorization, you may submit a copyright infringement complaint.
  • Copyright owners or their authorized representatives should provide sufficient information to identify the original copyrighted work and the allegedly infringing material.
  • All complaints should be submitted honestly and in good faith.

  1. Required Information for Copyright Complaints

  • To help us process your complaint, please include the following information:
  • Copyrighted Work: A description or identification of the original copyrighted material.
  • Infringing URL: The exact URL of the page containing the allegedly infringing content.
  • Contact Details: Your full name, email address and relevant contact information.
  • Authorization: Confirmation that you are the copyright owner or authorized to represent the copyright owner.
  • Good Faith Statement: A statement that you believe the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its representative or applicable law.
  • Accuracy Statement: Confirmation that the information provided is accurate.
  • Signature: Your physical or electronic signature.

  1. How to Submit a Copyright Complaint

  • Please submit your complaint through our official Contact Us page or designated copyright communication channel.
  • Use the subject line ‘Copyright Infringement Complaint’ and include the relevant URLs and supporting information.
  • If additional information is required, we may contact you for clarification.
  • Incomplete complaints may take longer to review.

  1. Review and Removal of Content

  • Upon receiving a valid and sufficiently detailed complaint, we will review the reported content and the information provided.
  • Where appropriate, we may remove, disable access to or modify the disputed material.
  • We may also contact the relevant content contributor or affected party for additional information.
  • We aim to review legitimate complaints within a reasonable timeframe, depending on the nature and completeness of the request.

  1. Counter-Notification

  • If you believe that content was removed or restricted due to a mistake or misidentification, you may contact us with an explanation and relevant supporting information.
  • A counter-notification should identify the removed material, its previous location, your reason for disputing the removal and your contact details.
  • Where applicable, formal DMCA counter-notification requirements under relevant United States copyright law may apply.
  • We will review counter-notifications and take appropriate action in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

  1. Repeat Copyright Infringement

  • YOJANA PORTAL does not support repeated or intentional copyright infringement.
  • Where appropriate, we may take action against repeated violations, including content removal or restrictions on relevant publishing privileges.
  • Any such action will depend on the circumstances and applicable legal requirements.

  1. False Copyright Claims

  • Copyright complaints should only be submitted by the actual copyright owner or an authorized representative.
  • Knowingly submitting false, misleading or fraudulent copyright complaints may result in legal consequences.
  • We reserve the right to disregard complaints that are incomplete, abusive or unsupported by sufficient information.

  1. Third-Party Websites and Content

  • YOJANA PORTAL may contain links to external websites, official government portals and third-party resources.
  • We do not own or control content hosted on external websites and are not responsible for their copyright practices.
  • If copyrighted material is hosted on another website, the copyright owner may need to contact the respective website or hosting provider directly.

  1. Important Legal Information

  • This policy is intended to establish a transparent process for handling copyright-related concerns.
  • The Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) is a United States copyright law. Its specific statutory procedures may not apply to every situation or jurisdiction.
  • Copyright complaints involving YOJANA PORTAL will be considered in accordance with applicable laws and the circumstances of each case.
  • Nothing in this policy limits any rights or remedies available under applicable copyright laws.

  1. Contact Us

  • For copyright complaints, content removal requests or questions regarding this policy, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
  • Subject: Copyright Infringement Complaint
  • Website: YOJANA PORTAL
  • Please provide the relevant page URL and sufficient details to help us review your request.

  1. Changes to This Policy

  • We may update this DMCA Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website practices, copyright procedures or applicable legal requirements.
  • Any revisions will be published on this page.
  • Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.
