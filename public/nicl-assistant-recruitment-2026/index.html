📝 How to Apply - NICL Assistant Vacancy 2026

  1. Visit Official Website: Open the official NICL recruitment portal and access the Assistant Recruitment 2026 application link.
  2. Complete Registration: Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  3. Login to Your Account: Log in with your Registration Number and Password.
  4. Fill Application Form: Enter your personal, educational, communication, and other required details accurately.
  5. Select State/UT: Choose the preferred State/UT for which you are applying.
  6. Upload Documents: Upload your photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and handwritten declaration in the prescribed format.
  7. Check Application Details: Preview the completed application and verify all information carefully before submission.
  8. Pay Application Fee: Pay the applicable fee online using the available payment methods.
  9. Submit Application Form: Complete the final submission after verifying the details and successful fee payment.
  10. Save and Print Confirmation: Download and print the submitted application form and fee payment receipt for future reference.
