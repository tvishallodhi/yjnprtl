Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC)

UPTET February 2027 Exam


Important Dates

  • Registration Start Date : 24/11/2026
  • Last Date to Submit Application Fee : 23/12/2026
  • Last Date for Fee Payment : 23/12/2026
  • Application Correction Last Date : 27/12/2026
  • UPTET Exam Date : 26/02/2027 & 27/02/2027

Application Fee

  • General / OBC / EWS: ₹1000 (Per Paper)
  • SC / ST: ₹500 (Per Paper)
  • PwD (Divyang): ₹300 (Per Paper)
  • Payment can be made online through UPI, net banking, debit/credit card, etc.

Age Limit

  • There is no specific age limit prescribed for UPTET February 2027

Age relaxation applicable as per official state rules.

🧍 UPTET Notification 2027 - Eligibility

  • Paper 1 – Primary Level (Class 1 to 5):
  • Graduation with 2-year BTC / D.El.Ed.
  • Class 12th with at least 50% marks and 4-year B.El.Ed.
  • Paper 2 – Upper Primary Level (Class 6 to 8):
  • Graduation with 2-year BTC / D.El.Ed. / B.Ed.
  • Class 12th with at least 50% marks and 4-year B.El.Ed.
  • Class 12th with at least 50% marks and 4-year B.A.-B.Ed. / B.Sc.-B.Ed.
  • For detailed eligibility criteria, please read the official notification carefully.

Documents Required

  • Recent Passport Size Photograph
  • Scanned Signature
  • Aadhaar Card
  • 10th & 12th Marksheet
  • Graduation Marksheet / Degree
  • B.Ed. / D.El.Ed. Certificate
  • Valid Mobile Number & Email ID
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit Official Website: Open the UPESSC official website at upessc.up.gov.in/.
  2. One Time Registration (OTR): Complete the OTR process using the required personal details.
  3. Login: Log in using your OTR registration details.
  4. Fill Application Form: Enter your personal, educational and communication details.
  5. Select Paper: Choose Paper 1 (Class 1 to 5), Paper 2 (Class 6 to 8), or both papers.
  6. Upload Documents: Upload your photograph, signature and other required documents in the prescribed format.
  7. Pay Application Fee: Submit the applicable examination fee through the available online payment mode.
  8. Final Submission: Review all entered details, submit the application form and download the confirmation page for future reference.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

❓ When will the UPTET 2027 online application form start?

The UPTET online registration and application process will start on 24 November 2026.

❓ What is the last date to apply for UPTET 2027?

The last date to submit the online application fee is 23 December 2026.

❓ What is the UPTET 2027 examination date?

The UPTET examination will be conducted on 26 and 27 February 2027.

❓ What are the two papers in UPTET 2027?

UPTET consists of two papers: Paper 1 for Primary Level (Class 1 to 5) and Paper 2 for Upper Primary Level (Class 6 to 8).

❓ Is One Time Registration (OTR) mandatory for UPTET 2027?

Yes, candidates must complete One Time Registration (OTR) before applying for UPTET.

❓ What is the last date for correction in the UPTET 2027 application form?

The last date for making corrections in the online application form is 27 December 2026.
