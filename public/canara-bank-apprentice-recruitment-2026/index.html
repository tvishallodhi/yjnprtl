🧍 Canara Bank Apprentice Vacancy 2026 - Eligibility

  • Candidates must possess a Graduation Degree in any discipline from a recognized university or an equivalent qualification recognized by the Central Government.
  • Graduation must have been completed between 01 January 2023 and 01 September 2026 (both dates inclusive).
  • Candidates must have secured at least 60% marks in Class 12th (55% for SC/ST/PwBD candidates) or Diploma Examination for the merit-based selection.
  • Candidates who have already undergone apprenticeship training or have one or more years of job experience after completing their educational qualification are not eligible.

📝 How to Apply - Canara Bank Apprentice Online Form 2026

  1. Open Recruitment Portal: Go to the official Canara Bank website and find the Graduate Apprentice Recruitment 2026 link.
  2. Register Yourself: Provide your basic information, mobile number and email ID to generate registration credentials.
  3. Sign In: Access the online application portal using your registration number and password.
  4. Provide Information: Enter your personal, educational, category and address details in the application form.
  5. Choose Your State: Select the state you want to apply for, keeping in mind that only one state is permitted.
  6. Update NATS Profile: Complete your NATS registration and make sure your profile status reaches 100%.
  7. Submit Scanned Documents: Upload your photograph, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration in the required format.
  8. Make Fee Payment: Complete the online payment of the application fee, wherever applicable.
  9. Check Application: Verify all the information carefully before clicking the final submit button.
  10. Keep Form Copy: Save and print the final application form and payment receipt for your records.

🙋‍♀️ Canara Bank Apprentice Notification 2026 - Faqs

❓ How many vacancies are available in Canara Bank Apprentice Recruitment 2026?

A total of 3,500 Graduate Apprentice vacancies have been announced by Canara Bank.

❓ What is the educational qualification required for Canara Bank Apprentice 2026?

Candidates must have a graduation degree in any discipline from a recognized university. Graduation must be completed between 01/01/2023 and 01/09/2026.

❓ What is the age limit for Canara Bank Apprentice Recruitment 2026?

The minimum age is 20 years and the maximum age is 28 years as on 01/09/2026. Applicable age relaxation is available for reserved categories.

❓ What is the monthly stipend for Canara Bank Apprentices?

Selected apprentices will receive a monthly stipend of ₹16,650, including the bank’s share and government DBT.

❓ What is the selection process for Canara Bank Apprentice Recruitment 2026?

Selection is based on 12th Standard or Diploma marks, followed by a local language test where applicable, document verification and medical fitness.
