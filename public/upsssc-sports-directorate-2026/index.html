🎓 UPSSSC Sports Directorate Vacancy 2026 - Vacancy & Qualification Details

Post NameTotal PostsEligibility Criteria
UPSSSC Sports Directorate108

Must meet the UPSSSC PET-2025 eligibility requirements.

  • Grounds Man: High School/equivalent + practical grounds/court maintenance experience.
  • Deputy Sports Officer: Graduation + relevant Coaching Diploma + 2 years coaching experience.
  • Assistant Coach: Graduation + relevant Coaching Diploma + 2 years coaching experience.
  • Life Guard: High School/equivalent + 3 years practical Life Guard experience.
  • Preferential Qualification: 1 year sports competition experience, Physical Education Diploma/Certificate, 2 years Territorial Army service, or NCC ‘B’ Certificate.
  • Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully for detailed eligibility criteria before applying.

🧍 UPSSSC Sports Directorate Vacancy 2026 - Eligibility

  • PET-2025: Candidates must fulfill the eligibility requirements based on UPSSSC PET-2025 as specified in the recruitment notification.
  • Grounds Man: High School or equivalent qualification, practical experience in preparing and marking grounds/courts for indoor and outdoor games, and knowledge of different types of grass, plants, flowers, seeds, planting, transplantation and related work. Pasted markdown
  • Deputy Sports Officer: Graduation from a recognized university, Diploma in Coaching in the relevant sport from a recognized institute/equivalent institution, and 2 years of coaching experience. Pasted markdown
  • Assistant Coach (Swimming): Graduation, Diploma in Coaching in the relevant sport from a recognized institute/equivalent institution, and 2 years of coaching experience. Pasted markdown
  • Life Guard: High School or equivalent qualification and at least 3 years of practical experience as a Life Guard. Pasted markdown
  • Assistant Coach (Cricket, Football, Shooting, Weightlifting, Fencing, Wrestling, Hockey, Table Tennis, Handball, Wushu, Lawn Tennis): Graduation, Diploma in Coaching in the relevant sport from a recognized institute/equivalent institution, and 2 years of coaching experience. Pasted markdown
  • Preferential Qualification: Preference may be given to candidates having 1 year of experience in organizing sports competitions, a Diploma/Certificate in Physical Education, at least 2 years of service in the Territorial Army, or an NCC ‘B’ Certificate. Pasted markdown

  • Educational Qualification Certificate / Mark Sheet
  • Relevant Sports Coaching Diploma / Certificate
  • Relevant Work Experience Certificate, wherever applicable
  • Sports Competition Experience Certificate, if applicable

  • Recent Passport-Size Photograph

  • Valid ID Proof
  • Other documents/certificates as required for the selected post and category

📝 How to Apply - UPSSSC Sports Directorate Vacancy 2026

  1. Visit Official Website: Go to the official UPSSSC website and open the application link for Sports Directorate Recruitment 2026.
  2. Login Using PET Details: Enter your UPSSSC PET-2025 Registration Number, Date of Birth and other required credentials.
  3. Check Candidate Information: Verify the displayed personal details and complete any additional information required in the application.
  4. Choose Recruitment Post: Select the Sports Directorate post you wish to apply for after confirming the post-wise eligibility criteria.
  5. Provide Qualification Details: Enter your educational qualification, relevant coaching diploma, work experience, sports details and other required information.
  6. Upload Required Documents: Upload your photograph, signature and applicable certificates/documents according to the prescribed specifications.
  7. Complete Fee Payment: Pay the ₹25 online processing fee through the available payment method and confirm successful payment.
  8. Review and Submit Form: Check all application details carefully, submit the form, and save or print the final application and fee receipt for future reference.
