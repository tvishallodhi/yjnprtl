🎓 SIDBI Recruitment 2026 - Vacancy & Qualification Details

Post NameTotal PostsEligibility Criteria
Assistant Manager Grade A – General Stream53

Graduation or professional qualification with the prescribed marks, along with relevant post-qualification work experience. Candidates holding CA or a recognised 2-year full-time MBA/PGDM are exempted from the prescribed work experience requirement.

  • Graduation in Commerce, Economics, Mathematics, Statistics, Business Administration or Engineering with minimum 60% marks (50% for SC/ST/PwBD candidates, wherever vacancies are reserved for them).
  • OR Company Secretary (CS), Cost and Management Accountant (CMA), Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) or Chartered Accountant (CA).
  • OR 2-year full-time MBA/PGDM in any discipline from a UGC-recognised or Government-approved institution.
  • Generally, at least 2 years of post-qualification experience as an Officer in lending, credit appraisal, risk management, treasury, audit, compliance, venture capital, private equity or debt financing/investment.
  • Alternatively, 2 years of relevant experience in specified SEBI-registered investment/merchant banking entities, AIFs, RBI-registered ARCs, bank subsidiaries dealing in MSME segments, eligible Government project consultancy/advisory firms or DPIIT-recognised BFSI startups.
  • Alternatively, at least 3 years of post-qualification experience as a Clerk/equivalent Class III employee in the specified financial and banking functions.
  • Eligible experience must be from the institutions and organisations specified in the official notification. Experience exclusively in personal, educational, vehicle or housing loans is not considered.
  • Recognised professional certifications such as CMA, CS, CFA, FRM, ACCA, Cloud Computing, Data Analytics, AI/ML and Cyber Security may be given preference as desirable qualifications.
Assistant Manager Grade A – Hindi Stream2

Bachelor's Degree with Hindi and English language subjects plus a translation diploma, or a relevant Master's Degree, along with at least 2 years of post-qualification work experience in Hindi translation or Official Language (Rajbhasha) work.

  • Bachelor's Degree from a recognised university with minimum 60% marks (55% for SC/ST/PwBD candidates, wherever vacancies are reserved for them), with both Hindi and English studied as compulsory/elective subjects for at least 2 years of the degree course.
  • AND a minimum 1-year Post-Graduate Diploma in Translation from Hindi to English and vice versa from a recognised university.
  • OR Master's Degree in Hindi with minimum 50% marks, with English studied as a main/elective subject for at least 2 years at Bachelor's Degree level.
  • OR Master's Degree in English with minimum 50% marks, with Hindi studied as a main/elective subject for at least 2 years at Bachelor's Degree level.
  • Candidates must be able to translate from English to Hindi and vice versa.
  • At least 2 years of post-qualification experience in glossary/terminology preparation, English-Hindi translation or implementation of Official Language (Rajbhasha) Policy.
  • Experience may be from Central/State Government departments, statutory/autonomous bodies, PSUs, Government-controlled institutions, Scheduled Commercial Banks, All India Financial Institutions, RBI-regulated NBFCs, FinTech companies, MFIs or national/state-level weekly publications/newspapers.
  • Published literary work(s) in Hindi and/or English is desirable.
Assistant Manager Grade A – Legal Stream3

Bachelor's Degree in Law (LLB) with prescribed marks, enrolment as an Advocate with the Bar Council of India and at least 2 years of post-qualification experience as a Law Officer.

  • Bachelor's Degree in Law from a UGC-recognised or Government-approved university/institution.
  • Minimum 50% marks (45% for SC/ST/PwBD candidates, wherever vacancies are reserved for them).
  • Must be enrolled as an Advocate with the Bar Council of India.
  • At least 2 years of post-qualification work experience as a Law Officer in Legal, Recovery and/or Commercial Contract Management functions.
  • Experience should include drafting, vetting, negotiation and management of commercial contracts and agreements.
  • Eligible experience organisations include All India Financial Institutions, Scheduled Commercial Banks, Central Public Sector Enterprises, RBI-regulated NBFCs, FinTech companies, MFIs, DRT, DRAT, NCLT and NCLAT.
  • Master's Degree in Law, qualification as a Company Secretary (CS), proficiency in computer applications and special knowledge of relevant laws are desirable.
Assistant Manager Grade A – Systems/Information Technology (IT)5

B.E./B.Tech. or MCA with prescribed marks, along with at least 2 years of post-qualification experience in Information Technology as an IT/Systems Officer.

  • B.E./B.Tech. in any engineering/technology discipline from a UGC-recognised or Government-approved university/institution with minimum 60% marks (55% for SC/ST/PwBD candidates, wherever vacancies are reserved for them).
  • OR MCA with minimum 60% marks (55% for SC/ST/PwBD candidates, wherever vacancies are reserved for them).
  • Preference for B.E./B.Tech. in Computer Science, Computer Technology, Information Technology, Electronics, Electronics and Communication Engineering or allied disciplines.
  • At least 2 years of post-qualification work experience in Information Technology as an IT/Systems Officer.
  • Eligible experience organisations include All India Financial Institutions, Scheduled Commercial Banks, large corporates and their subsidiaries listed on Indian or foreign exchanges, RBI-regulated NBFCs, FinTech companies and MFIs.
  • Relevant skill areas include AI/ML Automation, Full Stack Application Development, Security, Infrastructure and Network.
  • Industry certifications in AI/ML/Data Science, Full Stack Application Development, CISSP, CISM, CEH or ITIL Foundation are desirable.
Manager Grade B – Hindi Stream1

Bachelor's Degree with Hindi and English language subjects plus a translation diploma, or a relevant Master's Degree, along with at least 5 years of post-qualification work experience in Hindi translation or Official Language (Rajbhasha) work.

  • Bachelor's Degree from a recognised university with minimum 60% marks, with both Hindi and English studied as compulsory/elective subjects for at least 2 years of the degree course.
  • AND a minimum 1-year Post-Graduate Diploma in Translation from Hindi to English and vice versa from a recognised university.
  • OR Master's Degree in Hindi with minimum 50% marks, with English studied as a main/elective subject for at least 2 years at Bachelor's Degree level.
  • OR Master's Degree in English with minimum 50% marks, with Hindi studied as a main/elective subject for at least 2 years at Bachelor's Degree level.
  • Candidates must be able to translate from English to Hindi and vice versa.
  • At least 5 years of post-qualification work experience as a permanent employee or full-time direct contractual employee (not on third-party payroll) in glossary/terminology preparation, English-Hindi translation or implementation of Official Language (Rajbhasha) Policy.
  • Eligible experience organisations include RBI, SEBI, Central/State Government departments, statutory/autonomous bodies, PSUs, Government-controlled institutions, Scheduled Commercial Banks, All India Financial Institutions, RBI-regulated NBFCs, FinTech companies, MFIs and national/state-level weekly publications/newspapers.
  • Published literary work(s) in Hindi and/or English is desirable.
Manager Grade B – Legal Stream3

Bachelor's Degree in Law (LLB) with prescribed marks, enrolment as an Advocate with the Bar Council of India and at least 5 years of post-qualification experience as a Law Officer.

  • Bachelor's Degree in Law from a UGC-recognised or Government-approved university/institution.
  • Minimum 50% marks (45% for SC/ST/PwBD candidates, wherever vacancies are reserved for them).
  • Must be enrolled as an Advocate with the Bar Council of India.
  • At least 5 years of post-qualification work experience as a Law Officer on permanent or full-time direct contractual basis (not on third-party payroll).
  • Experience must relate to Legal, Recovery and/or Commercial Contract Management functions, including drafting, vetting, negotiation and management of commercial contracts and agreements.
  • Eligible experience organisations include RBI, SEBI, All India Financial Institutions, Scheduled Commercial Banks, Central Public Sector Enterprises, RBI-regulated NBFCs, FinTech companies, MFIs, DRT, DRAT, NCLT and NCLAT.
  • Master's Degree in Law, qualification as a Company Secretary (CS), proficiency in computer applications and special knowledge of Banking Law, Company Law, Labour Law, Constitutional Law, Recovery, Insolvency and Bankruptcy Laws, Property and Revenue matters are desirable.
Manager Grade B – Systems/Information Technology (IT)1

B.E./B.Tech. or MCA with prescribed marks, along with at least 5 years of post-qualification experience in Information Technology as an IT/Systems Officer and specified technical experience in the relevant skill area.

  • B.E./B.Tech. in any engineering/technology discipline from a UGC-recognised or Government-approved university/institution with minimum 60% marks (55% for SC/ST/PwBD candidates, wherever vacancies are reserved for them).
  • OR MCA with minimum 60% marks (55% for SC/ST/PwBD candidates, wherever vacancies are reserved for them).
  • Preference for B.E./B.Tech. in Computer Science, Computer Technology, Information Technology, Electronics, Electronics and Communication Engineering or allied disciplines.
  • Relevant skill areas include AI/ML Automation, Full Stack Application Development, Security, Infrastructure and Network.
  • At least 5 years of post-qualification work experience in Information Technology as an IT/Systems Officer on permanent or full-time direct contractual basis (not on third-party payroll).
  • Eligible experience organisations include RBI, SEBI, All India Financial Institutions, Scheduled Commercial Banks, large corporates and their subsidiaries listed on Indian or foreign exchanges, RBI-regulated NBFCs, FinTech companies, MFIs and eligible Mutual Fund/Asset Management Companies registered with SEBI.
  • For the AI/ML skill area, at least 2 years of experience in AI/ML solutions involving development of ML/Deep Learning algorithms and at least 2 years in Python/R programming are required.
  • OR for the Full Stack skill area, at least 2 years of experience in web application development and at least 2 years in RDBMS, SQL and PL/SQL programming are required.
  • OR 5 years of experience in IT Security Management.
  • OR 5 years of experience in IT Infrastructure and Network Management.
  • Relevant certifications in AI/ML/Data Science, Full Stack Application Development, CISSP, CISM, CEH or ITIL Foundation are desirable.

📝 How to Apply - SIDBI Grade a & B Recruitment 2026

  1. Select the official SIDBI recruitment website: Visit the official website and open the Assistant Manager Grade A / Manager Grade B Recruitment 2026 application link.
  2. New Registration: Click on New Registration and enter your name, mobile number, email ID and other required details.
  3. Login: Use your Registration Number and Password to log in to the application portal.
  4. Fill Personal Details: Enter your date of birth, gender, category, nationality, address and other personal information.
  5. Enter Educational Details: Fill in your educational qualifications, degree details, marks, passing year and relevant work experience.
  6. Upload Documents: Upload your recent photograph, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration in the prescribed format.
  7. Pay Application Fee: Pay the applicable application fee through Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking or other available online payment methods.
  8. Final Submit & Print: Review all entered details, submit the application form and download or print the final application form and payment receipt for future reference.

🙋‍♀️ SIDBI Assistant Manager Online Form 2026 - Faqs

❓ How many vacancies are available under SIDBI Recruitment 2026?

SIDBI Recruitment 2026 includes a total of 68 vacancies, comprising 63 posts for Assistant Manager Grade A and 5 posts for Manager Grade B.

❓ What are the post names and vacancies under Assistant Manager Grade A?

The stream-wise vacancy details are as follows:

General Stream – 53 Posts

Hindi Stream – 2 Posts

Legal Stream – 3 Posts

Systems / IT Stream – 5 Posts

❓ What is the educational qualification required for SIDBI Assistant Manager Recruitment 2026?

Candidates must possess the prescribed educational qualifications according to their respective streams. General Stream accepts eligible Graduation, CA, CS, CMA, CFA or Full-Time MBA/PGDM qualifications. Hindi, Legal and Systems/IT streams require relevant degrees and specialised qualifications.

❓ Is work experience required for SIDBI Assistant Manager Recruitment 2026?

Yes, relevant work experience is generally required for the posts. Assistant Manager Grade A positions usually require at least 2 years of relevant experience. Eligible CA and Full-Time MBA/PGDM candidates applying for the General Stream are exempted from the experience requirement. Manager Grade B positions have separate experience requirements.

❓ What is the age limit for SIDBI Assistant Manager Recruitment 2026?

For Assistant Manager Grade A, the minimum age is 21 years and the maximum age is 30 years. For Manager Grade B, the age limit is 25 to 33 years. Age relaxation is applicable to reserved categories as per the prescribed rules.

❓ What is the application fee for SIDBI Recruitment 2026?

The application fee, including the intimation charges, is:

General / OBC / EWS: ₹1,100

SC / ST / PwBD: ₹175

❓ What is the last date to apply for SIDBI Assistant Manager Recruitment 2026?

The last date to submit the online application form and pay the application fee is 11 December 2026. Candidates should complete the application process before the deadline.
