Cookie Policy – YOJANA PORTAL

This Cookie Policy explains how YOJANA PORTAL uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

Cookies help us improve website functionality, understand visitor interactions, maintain security and support services such as analytics and advertising.

By continuing to use our website, you acknowledge that cookies may be used as described in this policy, subject to applicable laws and your available cookie preferences.

  1. What Are Cookies?

  • Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites when you visit them. They help websites remember certain information about your visit, preferences and browsing activity.
  • Cookies may be temporary (session cookies) or remain on your device for a specified period (persistent cookies).
  • Similar technologies, such as pixels, tags and local storage, may also be used for related purposes.

  1. How We Use Cookies

  • YOJANA PORTAL may use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:
  • Website Functionality: To support essential website features and improve navigation.
  • User Preferences: To remember certain preferences, such as display settings or theme selections, where applicable.
  • Analytics: To understand website traffic, visitor interactions and content performance.
  • Advertising: To support the display, measurement and delivery of advertisements through third-party advertising partners.
  • Security: To help detect suspicious activity and maintain the security of our website.

  1. Types of Cookies We May Use

  • Essential Cookies: These may be necessary for certain website functions and basic operations.
  • Functional Cookies: These help remember user preferences and improve the browsing experience.
  • Analytics Cookies: These help us understand how visitors use our website, including which pages are visited and how users interact with content.
  • Advertising Cookies: These may be used by advertising partners to deliver, measure or personalize advertisements, depending on user preferences and applicable requirements.

  1. Third-Party Cookies

  • Some cookies may be placed by third-party services integrated into our website.
  • These may include analytics providers, advertising networks, embedded content providers and other external services.
  • Third-party services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and cookie practices. YOJANA PORTAL does not directly control all cookies placed by these providers.
  • Users should review the relevant third-party policies to understand how their information is collected and processed.

  1. Google Analytics and Advertising Services

  • YOJANA PORTAL may use Google Analytics or similar analytics services to understand website usage, visitor trends and content performance.
  • We may also use Google AdSense or other advertising networks to display advertisements on our website.
  • These services may use cookies or similar technologies to collect information about browsing activity, subject to their respective settings and policies.
  • For additional information, users can review the applicable privacy and cookie policies of the respective service providers.

  1. Managing and Disabling Cookies

  • Most web browsers allow users to view, manage, restrict or delete cookies through their settings.
  • You can configure your browser to block cookies or notify you when cookies are being placed on your device.
  • Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect some website features, preferences or functionalities.
  • For information about managing cookies, refer to the help or support section of your browser.

  1. Cookie Consent and Preferences

  • Where required by applicable laws, cookie consent or preference mechanisms may be provided to help users manage non-essential cookies.
  • Users may be able to change or withdraw their cookie preferences through the available controls, where implemented.
  • Essential cookies may continue to operate where they are necessary for the requested website functionality or otherwise permitted by applicable laws.

  1. Data Collected Through Cookies

  • Depending on the cookies and services used, information may include browser type, device information, approximate location, visited pages, referral sources and interaction data.
  • Cookies generally do not directly identify a person by name, but certain information may be associated with other data held by service providers.
  • For further details about how information is handled, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

  1. Changes to This Cookie Policy

  • We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, technology, advertising services or applicable requirements.
  • Any updates will be published on this page with the revised information.
  • Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.

  1. Contact Us

  • If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Cookie Policy or our use of cookies, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
