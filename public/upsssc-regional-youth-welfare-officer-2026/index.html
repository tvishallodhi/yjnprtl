🧍 UPSSSC Regional Youth Welfare Officer Vacancy 2026 - Eligibility

  • Regional Youth Welfare & Provincial Development Team Officer
  • Essential Educational Qualification:
    • Graduation degree from a recognized University established by law in India.
    • Any other equivalent qualification recognized by the Government will also be accepted.
  • Preferential Qualification:
    • B.P.Ed./D.P.Ed. from a recognized University/Institution.
    • 3 years of service as Block Commander, Halka Sardar or Dalapati in the organization.
    • At least 2 years of service in the Territorial Army.
    • NCC ‘B’ Certificate.
  • Exercise Trainer
  • Essential Educational Qualification:
    • Graduation degree from a recognized University established by law in India or an equivalent qualification recognized by the Government.
    • D.P.Ed./B.P.Ed. or an equivalent qualification recognized by the Government from a recognized University/Institution.
  • Preferential Qualification:
    • Diploma in the relevant branch of training/coaching from NIS Patiala, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Lakshmibai National Institute of Physical Education, Gwalior, or any other institution approved by the Government of India.
    • At least 2 years of service in the Territorial Army.
    • NCC ‘B’ Certificate.

👤 UPSSSC Regional Youth Welfare Officer Recruitment 2026 - Age Limit

  • Age Limit as on 01/07/2026
  • Minimum Age : 21 Years
  • Maximum Age : 40 Years

Age relaxation applicable as per official state rules.

📄 UPSSSC Youth Welfare Officer Online Form 2026 - Documents Required

  • PET 2025 Registration Number/Details: Required for the application process.
  • Educational Qualification Certificate: Graduation certificate/marksheet.
  • D.P.Ed./B.P.Ed. Certificate: Required for the Exercise Trainer post.
  • NCC ‘B’ Certificate: If claiming the preferential qualification.
  • Territorial Army Service Certificate: If applicable.
  • Relevant Training/Coaching Diploma: If applicable for Exercise Trainer.
  • Caste/Category Certificate: If applicable.
  • Domicile Certificate: If applicable.
  • Recent Passport-Size Photograph: As per prescribed specifications.
  • Signature: Scanned signature in the prescribed format.
  • Valid ID Proof: Aadhaar Card/PAN Card/Voter ID or other accepted ID.

📝 How to Apply - UPSSSC Youth Welfare Officer Apply Online 2026

  1. Visit the UPSSSC Official Website: Open the official UPSSSC website and go to the recruitment/application section.
  2. Open the Recruitment Link: Select the Regional Youth Welfare & Provincial Development Team Officer / Exercise Trainer Recruitment 2026 application link.
  3. Enter PET Details: Enter your UPSSSC PET 2025 Registration Number and other required PET details.
  4. Complete Registration: Verify your personal details and complete the registration/application process as instructed.
  5. Fill Application Form: Enter your educational qualification, category, address and other required information carefully.
  6. Upload Documents: Upload the required photograph, signature and other documents in the prescribed format.
  7. Pay Application Fee: Pay the applicable online processing/application fee through the available payment method.
  8. Submit & Print: Check all details carefully, submit the application form and save/print the final application for future reference.
