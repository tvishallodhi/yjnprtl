📝 How to Apply - SSC CHSL 12th Level Bharti 2026

  1. Official Website: Open the SSC official website at ssc.gov.in and navigate to the Login or Register section to begin.
  2. One-Time Registration: Register by entering your Aadhaar/identity information, personal details, date of birth, matriculation details, mobile number and email ID, then complete OTP verification.
  3. Login: Access your account using your Registration Number and Password, and select Apply for the Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination 2026.
  4. Educational Qualification: Provide your highest qualification and Class 12th details, including passing year, roll number, percentage/CGPA and Board/University name.
  5. Additional Details: Enter the required information about ex-serviceman status, age relaxation, declarations and other applicable details.
  6. Examination Centre: Select your preferred examination centres in the prescribed order of preference from the options available in your SSC Region.
  7. Photograph & Signature: Capture your live photograph through the application module and upload the required signature. Upload the applicable disability certificate, if required.
  8. Review & Submit: Check the application form carefully, pay the applicable ₹100 fee online, complete the final submission and save a copy of the submitted application for future reference.

🙋‍♀️ SSC CHSL 2026 - Faqs

❓ What is the SSC CHSL job salary?

The salary depends on the post and pay level. LDC/JSA posts come under Pay Level 2 (₹19,900–₹63,200), DEO posts under Pay Level 4 (₹25,500–₹81,100) or Level 5 (₹29,200–₹92,300), and DEO Grade A under Pay Level 4. The in-hand salary may vary based on allowances and deductions.

❓ Are SSC CHSL 2026 application forms out?

Yes. The SSC CHSL 2026 application process started on 7 September 2026. Eligible candidates can apply online until 7 October 2026 through the official SSC website.

❓ Is SSC CHSL very hard to crack?

The difficulty level depends on a candidate’s preparation, accuracy, and time-management skills. The exam covers English, General Intelligence, Quantitative Aptitude, and General Awareness. Regular practice and revision can help candidates prepare effectively.

❓ Is SSC CHSL very difficult for beginners?

Beginners may initially find some topics challenging, especially Mathematics and English. However, they can build their preparation by learning basic concepts, following the syllabus, solving previous-year papers, and taking mock tests.

❓ Will there be an SSC CHSL exam in 2026?

Yes. SSC has announced the CHSL 2026 recruitment examination. The exact examination dates are yet to be announced. Candidates should check the official SSC website for updates.

❓ Can I crack SSC CHSL in 3 months?

It is possible to prepare for SSC CHSL in three months, depending on your current preparation level, available study time, and consistency. Focus on completing the syllabus, solving previous-year papers, taking mock tests, and revising regularly. Selection cannot be guaranteed within a fixed preparation period.

❓ Who is eligible to apply for the SSC CHSL 2026 form?

Candidates who have passed Class 12 or an equivalent examination from a recognised board or university can apply for LDC/JSA and most DEO posts. For specific DEO/DEO Grade A posts, candidates must have passed Class 12 in the Science stream with Mathematics. The general age limit is 18 to 27 years as on 1 August 2026, with applicable age relaxation for eligible categories.
