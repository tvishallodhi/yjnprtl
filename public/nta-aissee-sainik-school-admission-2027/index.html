Age relaxation applicable as per official state rules.

🧍 Sainik School Admission 2027 - Eligibility

  • Class VI Admission
  • Candidate’s age must be between 10 and 12 years as on 31 March 2027.
  • Date of Birth must be between 01 April 2015 and 31 March 2017.
  • Both boys and girls are eligible to apply.
    1. Class IX Admission
  • Candidate’s age must be between 13 and 15 years as on 31 March 2027.
  • Date of Birth must be between 01 April 2012 and 31 March 2014.
  • Candidate must have passed Class VIII from a recognised school at the time of admission.
  • Girls are eligible subject to vacancy availability.

Documents Required

  • Recent Passport Size Photograph: JPG format, 10 KB to 200 KB.
  • Candidate's Signature: JPG format, 10 KB to 50 KB.
  • Left-Hand Thumb Impression: JPG format, 10 KB to 50 KB. Right-hand thumb impression can be used if the left thumb is unavailable.
  • Date of Birth Certificate: Issued by the competent authority.
  • Domicile Certificate: Required as applicable.
  • Caste / Community Certificate: For applicable category candidates.
  • Defence Service Certificate: For serving Defence personnel, if applicable.
  • PPO Certificate: For Ex-Servicemen, if applicable.
  • School Certificate: Principal's certificate confirming current study in an approved New Sainik School, where applicable.
  • Identity Proof: School ID Card, PAN Card, last four digits of Aadhaar, Passport or any other Government-issued Photo ID.
  • Mobile Number and Email ID: For OTP verification and registration.
  • Debit / Credit Card or Net Banking: For online application fee payment.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit Official Website: Open the NTA AISSEE official website at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society
  2. New Registration: Click on the AISSEE 2027 Registration link and complete the registration using your name, date of birth, mobile number and email ID.
  3. Login: Use the application number and password or other required credentials to log in to the application portal.
  4. Fill Application Form: Enter personal details, address, category, educational qualification and select the admission class (Class VI or Class IX).
  5. Upload Documents: Upload your recent photograph, signature and other required documents in the prescribed format.
  6. Select Exam Preferences: Choose your preferred examination cities and other required details as mentioned in the application form.
  7. Pay Application Fee: Pay the application fee online through the available payment methods and save the payment receipt.
  8. Final Submit & Print: Review all entered details, submit the application form and download or print the confirmation page for future reference.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

❓ What is the full form of AISSEE?

AISSEE stands for All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination.

❓ Which classes are covered under AISSEE 2027?

AISSEE 2027 is conducted for admission to Class VI and Class IX in Sainik Schools and approved New Sainik Schools.

❓ What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?

The last date to submit the online application form is 02 December 2026.

❓ What is the age limit for AISSEE 2027?

For Class VI, candidates must be 10 to 12 years old, and for Class IX, candidates must be 13 to 15 years old as on 31 March 2027.

❓ What is the application fee for AISSEE 2027?

The application fee is ₹850 for General, OBC-NCL, Defence and Ex-Servicemen candidates, and ₹700 for SC/ST candidates.

❓ When will the AISSEE 2027 examination be conducted?

The AISSEE 2027 entrance examination is scheduled to be held on 31 January 2027.
