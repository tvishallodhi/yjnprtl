🧍 Supreme Court Court Master Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility

  • Candidates must be Indian citizens.
  • Must hold a Bachelor’s degree from a recognized University in India.
  • Must have English shorthand proficiency of 120 words per minute.
  • Must have computer typing speed of 40 words per minute.
  • Must have a minimum of 5 years’ regular service as Private Secretary/Senior PA/PA/Senior Stenographer in Government/Public Sector Undertakings/Statutory Bodies.

👤 SCI Court Master Recruitment 2026 - Age Limit

  • Age Limit as on 01/10/2026
  • Minimum Age : 30 Years
  • Maximum Age : 45 Years

Age relaxation applicable as per official state rules.

📄 SCI Court Master Online Form 2026 - Documents Required

  • Recent Passport Size Photograph: Scanned copy of recent photograph.
  • Signature: Scanned copy of the candidate’s signature.
  • Class 10th Certificate: Class X certificate for verification of name, father’s name, mother’s name and date of birth.
  • Class 12th Certificate: Class XII certificate.
  • Graduation Certificate: Bachelor’s degree certificate.
  • Experience Certificate: Proof of the required 5 years’ regular service.
  • Caste/Community Certificate: Required for candidates claiming reservation benefits.
  • Non-Creamy Layer Certificate: Required for eligible OBC-NCL candidates.
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC): Required from the employer for candidates working in Central/State Government Departments, Nationalized Banks, PSUs, etc.

📝 How to Apply - Supreme Court of India Court Master Recruitment 2026

  1. Visit the official Supreme Court of India website and open the Recruitment section: www.sci.gov.in
  2. Open the Court Master (Shorthand) Recruitment 2026 online application link and read the instructions carefully: Ensure you meet all prescribed eligibility conditions before applying.
  3. Enter your personal and educational details in the online application form: Fill all particulars correctly as per your Class X/High School certificate and other documents.
  4. Upload the required documents: Upload your recent photograph, signature, Class X, Class XII and Graduation certificates, Experience Certificate and caste/community certificate where applicable.
  5. Preview the completed application form: Check all details carefully because the application cannot be withdrawn or modified after submission.
  6. Pay the application fee online: Pay ₹1,500 for General/Unreserved candidates or ₹750 for SC/ST/OBC-NCL/Ex-Servicemen candidates, plus applicable bank charges.
  7. Submit the application form online: Complete the final submission before 15/11/2026 at 11.55 PM and retain the application number for future use.
  8. Take a printout of the submitted application form: Keep it safely for your records; the printout does not need to be sent to the Supreme Court Registry.
