🧍 UPSSSC Junior Engineer Mechanical Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility

  • UPSSSC PET-2025 Score Card is required.
  • Three-year Diploma in Mechanical Engineering from the Board of Technical Education, Uttar Pradesh, or an equivalent Diploma in Mechanical Engineering recognized by the Government.
  • Intermediate (12th) or equivalent examination, along with a three-year Diploma in Mechanical Maintenance Trade from a Government-recognized institution.
  • Diploma in Automobile / Mechanical Engineering from a recognized Polytechnic.
  • Three-year Diploma in Automobile or Mechanical Engineering (Automobile) from a recognized Polytechnic.
  • Diploma in Mechanical Engineering from an institution or university established by law in India, or Diploma issued by the Board of Technical Education, Uttar Pradesh, or National Certificate in Mechanical Engineering issued by AICTE.
  • Diploma in Mechanical Engineering from a recognized institution.
  • High School (10th) or equivalent examination, along with a three-year Diploma in Mechanical Engineering from the Board of Technical Education, Uttar Pradesh, or an equivalent qualification recognized by the Government.
  • Preferential qualification where prescribed: Minimum 2 years’ service in the Territorial Army or NCC ‘B’ Certificate.
  • For the applicable post, Heavy Vehicle Driving Licence is a preferential qualification.
  • Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully for detailed eligibility criteria before applying.

📝 How to Apply - UPSSSC Combined JE Mechanical Recruitment 2026

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website: Open the UPSSSC official website and go to the online application section for Advertisement No. 24-Exam/2026.
  2. Login with PET Details: Enter your UPSSSC PET-2025 registration/login details and verify your PET-2025 Score Card information.
  3. Select the Post: Select the eligible post under the Combined Junior Engineer (Mechanical) Main Examination (PET-2025)/24.
  4. Enter Personal Details: Fill in your name, date of birth, category, address, contact number and other required personal information.
  5. Enter Qualification Details: Fill in your educational qualification and other post-specific eligibility details exactly as mentioned in your certificates.
  6. Upload Documents: Upload the required photograph, signature, PET-2025 Score Card and other documents in the prescribed format.
  7. Pay Application Fee: Pay the applicable online application processing fee through the available online payment method and complete the fee reconciliation if required.
  8. Submit and Print: Review all entered information carefully, submit the application form and download or print the final submitted application for future reference.
