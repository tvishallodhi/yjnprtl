Short Information

Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has officially announced the recruitment of 507 Subedar and Sub Inspector posts in the MP Police Department.

Interested and eligible male and female candidates can submit online applications between 09/09/2026 and 23/09/2026 through the official MPESB portal.

Important Dates

  • Notification Date : 30/07/2026
  • Begin Date : 09/09/2026
  • Last Date : 23/09/2026
  • Payment Last Date : 23/09/2026
  • Correction Window : 09/09/2026 to 28/09/2026
  • Admit Card : To be notified soon
  • Exam Date : 28/10/2026

Application Fee

  • General / Other State: ₹500/- (Portal charge extra: ₹60 for Kiosk / ₹20 for Citizen Login)
  • SC / ST / OBC / EWS (MP Domicile Only): ₹250/- (Portal charge extra: ₹60 for Kiosk / ₹20 for Citizen Login)
  • Payment can be made online through UPI, Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, or MP Online Kiosks.

Age Limit

  • Age Limit as On 01/01/2026
  • Minimum Age : 18 Years
  • Maximum Age : 33 Years (Male
  • Maximum Age : 38 Years (Female

Age relaxation applicable as per official state rules.

Eligibility

  • Graduation Degree or 3-Year Engineering Diploma in relevant trade/stream from a recognized university or board.

Post-Wise / Category-Wise Vacancy Distribution

  • Subedar: 81 Posts
  • Sub Inspector (District Police Force - DEF): 312 Posts
  • Sub Inspector (Special Armed Force - SAF): 69 Posts (Male Only)
  • Sub Inspector (Technical Branches - Arms, Photo, QD, Finger Print): 45 Posts
  • Total Vacancies: 507 Posts

Physical Standards & Test (PMT / PET)

  • Height (Male): 167.5 cm minimum
  • Height (Female): 152.4 cm minimum
  • Chest (Male): 81 cm (unexpanded) to 86 cm (expanded) with minimum 5 cm expansion
  • Physical Efficiency Test (PET): 800m Run, Long Jump, and Shot Put (Gola Phenk)

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-sized color photograph
  • Scanned signature
  • Class 10th marksheet / certificate (for Date of Birth proof)
  • Class 12th marksheet
  • Graduation Degree / Diploma marksheets and passing certificates
  • MP State Employment Exchange (Rojgar Panjiyan) Registration
  • MP Permanent Domicile Certificate (if claiming reservation/concession)
  • Caste / Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS, if applicable)
  • Valid Government Photo ID Proof (PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License, Passport, etc.)

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official website: Open esb.mp.gov.in and select your preferred language.
  2. Read the latest notification: Review the complete rulebook for Subedar & Sub-Inspector Cadre Recruitment 2026.
  3. Go to the Candidate Online Form section: Click on the online application link on the MP Online / MPESB portal.
  4. Complete the registration: Create or log in with your MP Online Citizen Profile ID and link your MP Rojgar Panjiyan details.
  5. Fill out the application form: Enter personal details, select post preferences (DEF, SAF, Subedar, or Technical), and upload scanned documents.
  6. Pay the application fee: Complete payment via Net Banking, UPI, Debit/Credit Card, or authorized Kiosk.
  7. Print the application form: Download and retain a printed copy of the final application submission receipt for future reference.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the starting date to apply for MP SI Subedar 2026? Online applications open on 09 September 2026.
