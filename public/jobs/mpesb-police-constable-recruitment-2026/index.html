Post Date / Update:

Short Information:
Madhya Pradesh Employee Selection Board (MPESB) has released an Official Notification MP Police Constable Notification 2026 of Madhya Pradesh Police Constable. The application process will be open from 22 Sep to 06 Oct 2026. Candidates must meet the required eligibility criteria including . As per the official notification, MPESB is conducting recruitment for 7,500 Police Constable posts for eligible male and female candidates.. Interested candidates are advised to check the important dates and ensure they apply before the last date. For complete information, download the official notification and apply online using the links provided below.

Important Dates

  • Application Start Date : 22/09/2026
  • Last Date to Apply : 06/10/2026

  • Correction Last Date : 11/10/2026
  • Admit Card Release : Notify Soon
  • Exam Start Date : 19/11/2026

Application Fee

  • General / Other State Candidates: ₹560/-
  • SC / ST / OBC / PwD (MP State Only): ₹310/-
  • Portal Charge Rs. 60/– Include This Fee
  • The examination fee can be paid via MP Online authorized kiosk centers in cash or through Debit Card, Credit Card, and Net Banking.

📝 How to Apply - MPESB Police Recruitment Online Form

  1. Open the MPESB official recruitment website.
  2. Find the MP Police Constable Recruitment 2026 application link.
  3. Read the recruitment notification and verify all eligibility conditions.
  4. Complete the registration process using your valid mobile number and email ID.
  5. Log in with your registration details.
  6. Enter the required personal, educational and other information in the application form.
  7. Upload your photograph, signature and necessary documents as per the prescribed format.
  8. Carefully check the complete application form before submission.
  9. Pay the applicable examination fee through the available payment options.
  10. Submit the application form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
