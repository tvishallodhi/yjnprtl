📝 How to Apply - UPSSSC JE Online Form 2026

  1. PET Registration Details: Keep your UPSSSC PET 2025 registration number, score card and other required details ready before starting the application.
  2. Online Registration: Visit the official UPSSSC website and open the Combined Junior Engineer (Electrical) Recruitment 2026 online application link.
  3. Candidate Login: Login using your PET 2025 registration details and complete the candidate authentication process.
  4. Personal Details: Verify and fill in your name, date of birth, category, address and other required personal information carefully.
  5. Educational Details: Enter your Electrical Engineering diploma/qualification details and other post-specific eligibility information as applicable.
  6. Document Upload: Upload the required photograph, signature and other documents/certificates in the prescribed format.
  7. Application Fee: Pay the applicable application/processing fee through the available online payment method and verify the payment status.
  8. Final Submission: Preview the complete application form, check all details carefully, submit the form and take a printout/save the final application form for future reference.
