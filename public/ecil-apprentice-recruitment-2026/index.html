🧍 ECIL Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility

  • Indian residents are eligible to apply.
  • GEA: B.E./B.Tech. in relevant engineering branches, passed on or after 01/04/2024.

📝 How to Apply - ECIL GEA & TA Recruitment 2026

  1. NATS Registration: Visit the official NATS portal (nats.education.gov.in) and complete your registration.
  2. Visit Website: Open the official ECIL website (ecil.co.in).
  3. Career Section: Go to Careers → Current Job Openings and find the Apprentice Recruitment 2026 link.
  4. Apply Online: Click on the online application link and fill in the required details.
  5. Enter Details: Provide your personal, educational and other required information correctly.
  6. Upload Documents: Upload the required documents and photograph, wherever asked.
  7. Submit Form: Review all entered details and submit the online application.
  8. Print Application: Download and print the submitted application form with the system-generated application serial number for future reference.

🙋‍♀️ ECIL Apprentice Online Form 2026 - Faqs

❓ How many vacancies are available in ECIL Apprentice Recruitment 2026?

A total of 242 apprentice seats are available, including 200 Graduate Engineer Apprentice (GEA) and 42 Technician (Diploma) Apprentice (TA) seats.

❓ What is the educational qualification required for ECIL Apprentice Recruitment 2026?

Candidates must have a B.E./B.Tech. degree for Graduate Engineer Apprentice (GEA) or a 3-year Diploma for Technician Apprentice (TA) in the relevant engineering branch. The qualification must be completed on or after 01 April 2024.

❓ What is the age limit for ECIL Apprentice Recruitment 2026?

The maximum age limit is 25 years as on 31 December 2026. Age relaxation is 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC (NCL) and 10 years for PwD candidates.

❓ What is the monthly stipend for ECIL Apprentices?

Graduate Engineer Apprentices (GEA) will receive ₹12,300 per month, while Technician (Diploma) Apprentices (TA) will receive ₹10,900 per month.

❓ What is the last date to apply for ECIL Apprentice Recruitment 2026?

The last date to submit the online application is 13 December 2026 (up to 11:59 PM).

❓ What is the selection process for ECIL Apprentice Recruitment 2026?

Selection will be based on document verification followed by merit preparation according to consolidated marks obtained in the qualifying B.E./B.Tech. or Diploma examination.
