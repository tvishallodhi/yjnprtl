📝 How to Apply - UP Headmaster Recruitment 2026

  1. OTR Registration: Complete the One Time Registration (OTR) process on the UPESSC official portal and generate your OTR number.
  2. Login: Login to the UPESSC portal using your OTR number and required credentials.
  3. Application Form: Select the UPESSC Headmaster Recruitment 2026 (Advt. No. 09/2026) and open the online application form.
  4. Personal Details: Enter your name, date of birth, category, address and other personal details carefully.
  5. Qualification Details: Enter your Post Graduation, B.Ed./B.P.Ed. and prescribed experience details as mentioned in the notification.
  6. Document Upload: Upload the required photograph, signature and other documents in the prescribed format and size.
  7. Fee Payment: Pay the application fee of ₹1,500 through the available online payment method and keep the payment receipt.
  8. Final Submission: Preview the complete application form, verify all details, submit the form and take a printout/save the application form for future reference.
