🧍 UGC NET JRF December 2026 - Eligibility

  • Master’s Degree: General/Unreserved/General-EWS candidates must have at least 55% marks in a Master’s degree or equivalent examination from a UGC-recognized university/institution.
  • Reserved Categories: OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD/PwBD and Third Gender candidates require at least 50% marks in the Master’s degree or equivalent examination.
  • Final-Year/Result Awaited: Candidates pursuing their Master’s degree, appearing in the final year, awaiting results, or whose qualifying examination has been delayed may apply provisionally.
  • Four-Year Bachelor’s Degree: Candidates with a 4-year/8-semester Bachelor’s degree must have 75% marks or equivalent grade. A 5% relaxation is available for SC/ST/OBC-NCL, Differently-Abled, EWS and other specified categories.
  • Four-Year Degree Candidates: Candidates qualifying NET through a four-year Bachelor’s degree are eligible for JRF and Ph.D. admission, but not for appointment as Assistant Professor.
  • Subject Eligibility: Candidates are advised to appear in the subject of their Post-Graduation. If their PG subject is not included in the NET subject list, they may appear in a related subject.
  • JRF Age Limit: For JRF, the candidate must be not more than 30 years old as on 01/12/2026. Up to 5 years’ relaxation is provided to specified categories, including OBC-NCL, SC/ST, PwD/PwBD, Third Gender and women applicants.
  • Assistant Professor/Ph.D.: The bulletin’s age-limit provision does not prescribe an upper age limit for Assistant Professor and Ph.D. admission.
  • Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully for detailed eligibility criteria before applying.

How to Apply Online

  1. Begin Application: Visit the official NTA UGC NET website and register with your own Email ID and active Mobile Number.
  2. Complete Registration: Use the available Meri Pehchaan/SSO option to complete registration and generate your application login credentials.
  3. Fill Application Form: Enter your personal, academic, category and other required details correctly, then note the system-generated Application Number.
  4. Upload Documents: Upload the live photograph, recent photograph, signature and applicable Disability/UDID certificate as per the prescribed file format and size.
  5. Review Information: Check all entered details and uploaded documents carefully to avoid errors before moving to the payment stage.
  6. Make Fee Payment: Pay the applicable examination fee through SBI/CANARA/UBI, Debit Card, Credit Card or UPI and retain the payment details.
  7. Submit Form: After successful fee payment, complete the final submission of the application form.
  8. Download Confirmation: Download and save the Confirmation Page after successful payment and take a printout for future reference.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

❓ What is the minimum educational qualification for UGC NET December 2026?

General/Unreserved/General-EWS candidates need at least 55% marks in a Master’s degree or equivalent, while OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD/PwBD and Third Gender candidates need 50% marks.

❓ Can final-year Master’s students apply for UGC NET December 2026?

Yes. Candidates pursuing their Master’s degree, appearing in the final year or awaiting results may apply provisionally, subject to the prescribed eligibility conditions.

❓ What is the age limit for UGC NET JRF December 2026?

The maximum age for JRF is 30 years as on 01/12/2026, with applicable age relaxation for specified categories and women candidates.

❓ Can candidates with a four-year Bachelor’s degree apply for UGC NET?

Yes. Eligible four-year Bachelor’s degree candidates can qualify for JRF and Ph.D. admission, but they are not eligible for appointment as Assistant Professor through this route.

❓ s there any upper age limit for Assistant Professor and Ph.D. admission?

No upper age limit is prescribed for Assistant Professor and Ph.D. admission.
