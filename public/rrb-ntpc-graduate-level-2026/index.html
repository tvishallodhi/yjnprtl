🧍 Railway Graduate Level Online Form 2026 - Eligibility

  • Educational Qualification: Candidates must have a Bachelor’s Degree from a recognized University or its equivalent qualification. The prescribed qualification must be possessed on or before the closing date of the application.
  • Final Year Candidates: Candidates who are waiting for the final result of the prescribed educational qualification are not eligible to apply. )
  • Post-wise Eligibility: The Graduate Level recruitment includes Chief Commercial Cum Ticket Supervisor, Goods Train Manager, Junior Accounts Assistant Cum Typist and Senior Clerk Cum Typist. The basic educational qualification for these notified posts is a Degree from a recognized University or its equivalent.
  • Medical Fitness: Candidates must satisfy the prescribed Railway medical standards for the post applied for. The posts carry different medical standards, including A-2, B-2 and C-2.

📝 How to Apply - RRB NTPC CEN 06/2026 Bharti Online Form

  1. Open the Official Website: Visit the official RRB website and locate the CEN 06/2026 NTPC Graduate Level recruitment section.
  2. Create Registration: Register on the application portal by providing the required basic details along with your mobile number and email address.
  3. Access the Application Form: Use your registration credentials to log in and open the RRB NTPC Graduate Level online form.
  4. Complete Candidate Details: Enter your personal information, date of birth, category, address and other details carefully as mentioned in your documents.
  5. Add Qualification Details: Provide your graduation information, university details, passing year and other required educational information.
  6. Upload Required Files: Upload the photograph, signature and other documents according to the prescribed specifications.
  7. Complete Fee Payment: Pay the applicable examination fee using UPI, net banking, debit card, credit card or another available online payment method.
  8. Final Submission: Check the complete application form carefully, submit it and save the final application form and payment receipt for future use.
