🙋‍♀️ MPESB Subedar and ASI Online Form 2026 - Faqs

❓ What is the selection process for MPESB ASI Steno Recruitment 2026?

The selection process includes a written examination, followed by document verification and practical/skill tests.

❓ How many vacancies are available in MPESB ASI Steno Recruitment 2026?

A total of 655 vacancies are available for Subedar (Ministerial) Stenographer and Assistant Sub-Inspector (Ministerial) posts.

❓ What is the educational qualification required for Subedar Stenographer posts?

Candidates must have passed 12th class, possess a CPCT Certificate with Hindi Typing, Hindi shorthand speed of 100 WPM, and the prescribed computer qualification.

❓ What is the qualification required for ASI Ministerial posts?

Candidates must have passed 12th class, hold a CPCT Certificate with Hindi Typing, and possess the prescribed computer qualification.

❓ What is the Hindi shorthand speed required for Subedar Stenographer?

Candidates applying for Subedar Stenographer posts must have Hindi shorthand speed of 100 words per minute (WPM).
