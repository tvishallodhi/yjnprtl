Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS)

Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Class 9th Admission 2027


Important Dates

  • Application Start Date : 02/09/2026
  • Last Date to Apply : 30/09/2026
  • Selection Test Date : 10/04/2027
  • Admit Card : Before Exam

Application Fee

  • Application Fee: ₹0
  • Online Application: Free of cost
  • Candidates can submit the application form online without paying any application fee.

Age Limit

  • Date of Birth : 01/05/2012 to 31/07/2014

Age relaxation applicable as per official state rules.

Eligibility

  • Candidate must be a bona fide resident of the concerned district where admission is sought.
  • Must be studying Class VIII during Academic Session 2026-27 in a Government/Government-recognized school of the same district where the JNV is located.
  • Date of Birth must be between 01 May 2012 and 31 July 2014 (both dates inclusive).
  • Only Indian Nationals studying Class VIII in India are eligible.
  • Candidates who have already passed Class VIII in an earlier academic session are not eligible.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit Official Website: Go to the official NVS website and open the Class IX Lateral Entry Selection Test 2027-28 application link.
  2. Check Eligibility: Read the prospectus carefully and ensure that you meet the district, Class VIII, nationality and date-of-birth eligibility requirements.
  3. Registration: Enter the required basic details such as State, District and other candidate information to complete the online registration.
  4. Fill Application Form: Enter the candidate's personal details, address, Class VIII study details, Aadhaar number and APAAR ID/PEN as required.
  5. Upload Documents: Upload the candidate's photograph, candidate's signature and parent's signature in JPG format with the prescribed file size.
  6. Verify Details: Carefully check all the information entered in the application form because the submitted details will be treated as final for further processing.
  7. Submit Form: Submit the completed application form online after verifying all details and keep the application details safely for future reference.
  8. Save Application: Download or print the submitted application form and keep it safely for use during the selection and admission process.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

❓ JNV Class 9th Admission 2027 के लिए कौन आवेदन कर सकता है?

वे विद्यार्थी जो शैक्षणिक सत्र 2026-27 में मान्यता प्राप्त स्कूल से कक्षा 8वीं में अध्ययन कर रहे हैं और संबंधित जिले के निवासी हैं, आवेदन कर सकते हैं।

❓ JNV Class 9th Admission 2027 के लिए आयु सीमा क्या है?

विद्यार्थी का जन्म 01 मई 2012 से 31 जुलाई 2014 के बीच होना चाहिए। दोनों तिथियां सम्मिलित हैं।

❓ क्या दूसरे जिले का विद्यार्थी आवेदन कर सकता है?

नहीं। विद्यार्थी उसी जिले का वास्तविक निवासी होना चाहिए जहां संबंधित जवाहर नवोदय विद्यालय स्थित है।

❓ क्या कक्षा 8वीं पहले पास कर चुके विद्यार्थी आवेदन कर सकते हैं?

नहीं। जो विद्यार्थी किसी पिछले शैक्षणिक सत्र में कक्षा 8वीं उत्तीर्ण कर चुके हैं, वे पात्र नहीं हैं।

❓ क्या केवल भारतीय विद्यार्थी आवेदन कर सकते हैं?

हाँ। केवल भारत में कक्षा 8वीं में अध्ययन कर रहे भारतीय नागरिक पात्र हैं।
