Editorial Policy

Learn how YOJANA PORTAL researches, verifies, prepares, and updates information about government schemes, jobs, examinations, admissions, and scholarships.

Editorial Policy

At YOJANA PORTAL, we aim to provide clear, useful, and reliable information about government schemes, jobs, recruitment, examinations, admissions, scholarships, results, and other public-interest updates.

This Editorial Policy explains how we prepare, verify, publish, and update content on our website. Our goal is to help readers understand important information while encouraging them to verify official details before taking action.

Our Editorial Principles

  • Accuracy: We make reasonable efforts to ensure that published information is correct and supported by reliable sources.
  • Clarity: We present information in a simple and understandable format so readers can easily find important details.
  • Transparency: We aim to identify official sources and distinguish available facts from general explanations.
  • Timeliness: We try to update content when relevant official information or changes become available.
  • Responsibility: We do not intentionally publish misleading information or make unsupported guarantees about schemes, jobs, admissions, or selection outcomes.

Sources of Information

  • We prioritize official sources such as government department websites, recruitment boards, examination authorities, official notifications, admission portals, and other authorized platforms.
  • We may also refer to press releases, official announcements, and publicly available documents to prepare informational content.
  • Where possible, we provide links to the relevant official notification or website so readers can independently verify the information.
  • If information is not confirmed through an official source, we aim to make its status clear and avoid presenting it as confirmed.

Content Research and Verification

  • Before publishing, we make reasonable efforts to review important details such as eligibility criteria, application dates, fees, vacancies, age limits, required documents, and application procedures.
  • Information may be checked against official notifications and relevant government or institutional websites.
  • When official information is incomplete, unclear, or subject to change, we may mention that readers should refer to the latest official announcement.
  • Readers should always consult the official notification for final and legally authoritative instructions.

Content Creation and Presentation

  • Our content is prepared to make public information easier to understand and navigate.
  • We may summarize or explain information from official documents, but we do not intend to replace the original notification, government order, or official instructions.
  • Headings, tables, lists, and other formatting may be used to help readers locate important information quickly.
  • We aim to avoid exaggerated headlines, misleading claims, and statements that could create false expectations.

Content Updates

  • We review published content when important changes are brought to our attention or when updated official information becomes available.
  • Updates may include changes to application dates, eligibility conditions, fees, vacancies, examination schedules, results, or official links.
  • Where appropriate, we may update the publication or modification date to reflect significant changes.
  • Some older pages may remain available for reference. Readers should check the latest official source before relying on time-sensitive information.

Corrections and Reader Feedback

  • We welcome feedback from readers who identify inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or unclear information.
  • If you notice an error, please contact us with the page URL and a brief explanation. Providing an official source or supporting document can help us review the issue.
  • We review reported issues and make corrections when appropriate. The time required may depend on the nature of the issue and the availability of supporting information.
  • For reporting an issue, please visit our Contact Us page.

Independence and Conflicts of Interest

  • Our editorial content is intended to provide information to readers. Advertising or other commercial relationships should not determine the factual presentation of editorial information.
  • If content is sponsored, promotional, or otherwise commercially influenced, we aim to identify it appropriately.
  • We do not claim to represent any government department, recruitment board, examination authority, or educational institution unless explicitly stated.

Advertising and Affiliate Content

  • YOJANA PORTAL may display advertisements or use third-party services to support website operations.
  • Advertisements are separate from editorial content. Their presence does not mean that YOJANA PORTAL endorses the advertised product, service, or organization.
  • If affiliate links or sponsored content are used, we aim to disclose them where applicable.

Use of Official Documents and External Links

  • We may link to official notifications, application portals, government websites, and other external resources for readers’ convenience.
  • External websites are managed by their respective owners. We are not responsible for changes, errors, availability, or content on those websites.
  • Readers should review the terms, instructions, and privacy policies of external websites before using them.

No Guarantee of Outcomes

  • YOJANA PORTAL provides informational content only. We do not guarantee selection, employment, admission, scholarship approval, scheme benefits, or any other outcome.
  • Eligibility and final decisions are determined by the relevant government department, recruitment authority, institution, or authorized organization.
  • Readers are responsible for checking official requirements and submitting accurate information through the appropriate official channels.
