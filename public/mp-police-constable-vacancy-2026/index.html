🙋‍♀️ MP Police Constable Notification 2026 - Faqs

❓ When will the MP Police Constable Recruitment 2026 take place?

The MPESB application process started on 22 September 2026. The written examination is scheduled to begin on 19 November 2026.

❓ How many marks are required to qualify for the MP Police Constable physical test?

Candidates must score at least 30 marks out of 100 (30%) in the Physical Efficiency Test (PET). These marks are added to the written examination score for the final merit list.

❓ What is the salary of an MP Police Constable?

The pay scale for Constable (GD) is ₹19,500–₹62,000. Applicable allowances and initial-year payment conditions are governed by the recruitment rules.

❓ How many vacancies are available in MP Police Constable Recruitment 2026?

There are 7,500 vacancies: 700 posts for Special Armed Force (SAF) and 6,800 posts for other Constable (GD) cadres.

❓ What is the exam pattern for the MP Police Constable Exam 2026?

The written examination consists of 100 objective questions for 100 marks, with a duration of 2 hours. The subjects are General Knowledge and Reasoning (40 marks), Intellectual Ability and Mental Aptitude (30 marks), and Science and Simple Mathematics (30 marks). There is no negative marking for incorrect answers.

❓ What is the last date to apply for the MP Police Constable Exam 2026?

The last date to submit the online application is 6 October 2026.

As per the MPESB Police Constable (GD) Recruitment Rule Book 2026, the written examination consists of three subjects carrying a total of 100 marks.
