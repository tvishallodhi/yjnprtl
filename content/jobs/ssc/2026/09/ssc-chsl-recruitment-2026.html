SSC CHSL Recruitment 2026 Short Syllabus Detail
Tier Subject Syllabus / Topics
Tier-I English Language Spot the Error, Fill in the Blanks, Synonyms/Homonyms, Antonyms, Spelling and Detecting Misspelt Words, Idioms & Phrases, One Word Substitution, Improvement of Sentences, Active/Passive Voice, Direct/Indirect Narration, Shuffling of Sentence Parts, Shuffling of Sentences in a Passage, Cloze Passage and Comprehension Passage.
General Intelligence Semantic Analogy, Symbolic Operations, Symbolic/Number Analogy, Trends, Figural Analogy, Space Orientation, Semantic Classification, Venn Diagrams, Symbolic/Number Classification, Drawing Inferences, Figural Classification, Punched Hole/Pattern Folding & Unfolding, Semantic Series, Figural Pattern Folding and Completion, Number Series, Embedded Figures, Figural Series, Critical Thinking, Problem Solving, Emotional Intelligence, Word Building, Social Intelligence, Coding and Decoding, Numerical Operations and other related topics.
Quantitative Aptitude Number Systems, Whole Numbers, Decimals, Fractions, Percentages, Ratio and Proportion, Square Roots, Averages, Simple and Compound Interest, Profit and Loss, Discount, Partnership, Mixture and Alligation, Time and Distance, Time and Work, Algebra, Elementary Surds, Graphs of Linear Equations, Geometry, Mensuration, Trigonometry, Heights and Distances, Standard Identities and Statistical Charts.
General Awareness Current Events, History, Culture, Geography, Economic Scene, General Policy and Scientific Research, particularly relating to India and its neighbouring countries.
Tier-II Mathematical Abilities Number Systems, Whole Numbers, Decimals, Fractions, Percentages, Ratio and Proportion, Square Roots, Averages, Simple and Compound Interest, Profit and Loss, Discount, Partnership, Mixture and Alligation, Time and Distance, Time and Work, Algebra, Elementary Surds, Graphs of Linear Equations, Geometry, Mensuration, Trigonometry, Heights and Distances, Standard Identities, Statistics and Probability, Mean, Median, Mode and Standard Deviation.
Reasoning and General Intelligence Semantic Analogy, Symbolic Operations, Symbolic/Number Analogy, Trends, Figural Analogy, Space Orientation, Semantic Classification, Venn Diagrams, Symbolic/Number Classification, Drawing Inferences, Figural Classification, Punched Hole/Pattern Folding & Unfolding, Semantic Series, Figural Pattern Folding and Completion, Number Series, Embedded Figures, Figural Series, Critical Thinking, Problem Solving, Emotional Intelligence, Word Building, Social Intelligence, Coding and Decoding, Numerical Operations and other related topics.
English Language and Comprehension Vocabulary, Grammar, Sentence Structure, Synonyms, Antonyms, Correct Usage, Spot the Error, Fill in the Blanks, Spelling, Idioms & Phrases, One Word Substitution, Improvement of Sentences, Active/Passive Voice, Direct/Indirect Narration, Shuffling of Sentence Parts, Shuffling of Sentences in a Passage, Cloze Passage and Comprehension Passage.
General Awareness Current Events, History, Culture, Geography, Economic Scene, General Policy, Scientific Research and general awareness of the environment and its application to society.
Computer Knowledge Test Computer Knowledge Test is a mandatory section of Tier-II and is qualifying in nature. Refer to the official notification for the detailed topics.
Skill Test / Typing Test Skill Test for Data Entry Operator (DEO) posts and Typing Test for LDC/JSA posts. This test is qualifying in nature.
