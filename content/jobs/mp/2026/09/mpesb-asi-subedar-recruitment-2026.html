MPESB ASI Steno & Subedar Stenographer Syllabus 2026

Stage 1: Written Examination

Subject Marks
General Knowledge & Logical Reasoning 40
Intellectual Ability & Mental Aptitude 30
Science & Simple Arithmetic 30
Total 100

Written Examination Pattern

Exam Mode Online (MCQ)
Total Questions 100
Total Marks 100
Duration 2 Hours
Correct Answer +1 Mark
Negative Marking No marks deducted for incorrect or unattempted questions
Question Paper Language Hindi

Stage 2: Practical Examination – Subedar Stenographer

Total Duration 1 Hour
Total Marks 100
Minimum Qualifying Marks 30 Marks
Hindi Shorthand Dictation Two dictations of 5 minutes each at 100 words per minute
Draft Review 5 minutes after each dictation to read and correct the draft
Hindi Transcription / Typing 20 minutes for each dictation
Shorthand Marks 10 marks for each dictation
Transcription Marks 40 marks for each transcription

Stage 2: Practical Examination – ASI Ministerial

Total Duration 1 Hour
Total Marks 100
Minimum Qualifying Marks 30 Marks
Hindi Typing Test Two paragraphs of 600 words each
Time 30 minutes for each paragraph
Marks 50 marks for each paragraph
Evaluation Typing accuracy, including omissions, additions, word/number substitutions, spelling errors and spacing mistakes

Note: The official rulebook specifies the subject-wise marks for the written examination and the practical-test requirements. It does not provide a detailed topic-wise breakdown for General Knowledge, Logical Reasoning, Mental Aptitude, Science or Simple Arithmetic in the syllabus section. Candidates should read the official notification carefully for complete instructions.
