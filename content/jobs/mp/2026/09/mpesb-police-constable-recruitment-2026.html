MP Police Constable Syllabus 2026

As per the MPESB Police Constable (GD) Recruitment Rule Book 2026, the written examination consists of three subjects carrying a total of 100 marks.

Subject Marks Important Topics for Preparation
General Knowledge and Logical Knowledge 40 General Knowledge of India and Madhya Pradesh, History, Geography, Indian Constitution, Current Affairs, General Awareness, Reasoning and Logical Questions.
Intellectual Ability and Mental Aptitude 30 Mental Ability, Analytical Thinking, Problem Solving, Decision Making, Similarities and Differences, Series and Pattern-Based Questions.
Science and Simple Arithmetic 30 General Science, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Science in Daily Life, Number System, Percentage, Ratio and Proportion, Average, Profit and Loss, and Basic Mathematics.
Total 100 Marks

MP Police Constable Exam Pattern 2026

Exam Type Objective Type (MCQ)
Total Questions 100
Total Marks 100
Exam Duration 2 Hours
Marks for Each Correct Answer 1 Mark
Negative Marking No Negative Marking

Note: The topics mentioned above are suggested areas for preparation. The official Rule Book specifies the subject names and marks distribution but does not provide a detailed topic-wise syllabus.
